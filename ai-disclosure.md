# AI Disclosure & Unfinished List

## AI Disclosure
* **Tools Used:** Utilized AI coding assistants (such as Gemini and Cursor) selectively as syntax references and autocomplete helpers for boilerplate structure, while retaining full ownership of the architecture, debugging, and core logic.
* **Concrete Example Where AI Was Suboptimal / Wrong:** When debugging the initial H2 database migration failure, the AI assistant suggested using a standard `AUTO_INCREMENT` primary key. Recognizing that H2 operating under `MODE=PostgreSQL` requires proper ANSI/PostgreSQL identity syntax, I rejected the AI's suggestion and manually implemented `BIGINT GENERATED BY DEFAULT AS IDENTITY PRIMARY KEY`. This demonstrated the critical need to independently verify and drive tool outputs against real runtime constraints.

## Unfinished List
* **Task 4 (Document Store Migration):** Could not complete the full migration of the ledger store from relational JDBC/H2 to a document store (DynamoDB/MongoDB), nor implement the custom backfill engine and consistency checker, due to the 48-hour time constraint. Priority was deliberately allocated to ensuring the absolute correctness, bug-free execution, and exact decimal precision of the core relational engine and SMS parser.
